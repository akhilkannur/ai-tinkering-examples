# SEO Strategy: Real AI Examples & Weekly Drops

**Target Audience:** Non-technical AI tinkerers, solo operators, and business owners looking for "practical edge" rather than developer APIs.

---

## 1. Core Keywords & Search Intent
Based on research via DataForSEO (April 2026), we focus on two distinct funnels:

### A. The "Examples" Funnel (Homepage)
*Intent: Discovery & Education ("What can I actually do with this?")*
- **Primary Keywords:** 
  - `AI use cases` (2,900 vol / Med difficulty)
  - `AI for business` (1,600 vol / Low difficulty)
  - `Real world AI` (480 vol / Low difficulty)
- **Winning Angle:** Practicality. While others list tools, we show the **workflow**.

### B. The "Tools" Funnel (Directory/Drops)
*Intent: Transactional ("Show me the new stuff.")*
- **Primary Keywords:**
  - `New AI tools` (High discovery intent)
  - `AI toolkit` (2,400 vol / Med difficulty)
  - `Free AI tools list` (880 vol / Low difficulty)
- **Winning Angle:** Recency & Maker Culture. We are the "Product Hunt" for AI tinkerers.

---

## 2. Brand Positioning: "The Weekly Drop"
We don't maintain a static database; we deliver **Weekly Drops**. 
- **The Hook:** Every Sunday publication.
- **The Filter:** Non-technical. If a "tinkerer" can't use it in 5 minutes, it doesn't make the drop.

---

## 3. On-Page SEO Guidelines

### Meta Title Patterns
- **Pattern:** `{Action/Frequency} AI {Format}: {Keyword} for {Audience}`
- **Example:** `Weekly AI Drops: New Tools & Real Use Cases for Tinkerers`

### Meta Description Patterns
- **Pattern:** `{Frequency}, we drop {Value Prop} from {Source}. {Tool Count} tools curated for {Audience}.`
- **Example:** `Every Sunday, we drop the latest AI tools from independent makers and real-world use cases. 343 tools curated for non-technical tinkerers.`

---

## 4. Content Strategy for Tinkerers
To maintain SEO authority, new blog posts or examples should target:
1. **"How to" for Business:** "How to use [Tool] for [Business Process]."
2. **"Pros/Cons":** Honest reviews of maker tools (low difficulty, high trust).
3. **Comparison Lists:** "Top 5 AI tools for [Specific Niche]" (e.g., Sales, Real Estate, Writing).

---

## 5. Maintenance Checklist
- [ ] **Sunday Updates:** Ensure `dateAdded` is updated so the "DROP / [DATE]" label reflects the most recent Sunday.
- [ ] **Dynamic Counts:** Always use the `aiTools.length` variable in meta tags to show the directory is growing.
- [ ] **Sponsor Tags:** Mark partner tools (like SalesTools.club) as `Sponsor` to maintain transparency and "Curated" trust.
